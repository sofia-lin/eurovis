How Eurovision Voting Worked

From 2016, Eurovision split the vote into two equal halves: a professional national jury and a public televote. Each voting country awarded points (1–8, 10, 12) separately from both the jury and the public.

  • Map — Finalist countries for the selected year. Gold = winner, blue = finalist. Click any country to explore.
  • Chord diagram — Jury voting flows between countries, coloured by geographic region. Thicker chords = more points. Click a country to highlight its flows.
  • Performance preview — YouTube embed of the selected country's Eurovision entry.
  • Parallel coordinates — Each line connects a country's jury points (left) to its public points (right). Steep crossings reveal where jury and public disagreed most.
